Family Reframed is a specialist family mediation service for couples and families navigating separation, divorce, parenting arrangements, and financial transitions — with a difference. We offer a structured process that regularly includes strategic input from therapists, accountants, lawyers, and other professionals, designed to bring clarity, reduce conflict, and support better decision-making.

Traditional legal processes often separate issues into different tracks — lawyers for legal matters, therapists for personal concerns, accountants for finances. This leads to fragmented advice and slower resolution. Family Reframed brings these elements into one coordinated process, so decisions are made with clarity and context, not in isolation.
